# BoardMaster usability study kit

Prepared 6 November 2026 with AI assistance. Status: ready for recruitment;
no participants, observations or success results have been recorded.

## Participants and session

Recruit five university students aged 18+ who are not team members. Participation
is voluntary; use anonymous IDs P01–P05 and do not collect names, student IDs or
contact details in the repository. Explain that model answers send questions and
rulebook excerpts to Alibaba Cloud. Do not enter personal information. Obtain
agreement to participate; ask separately before any recording. Participants may
stop at any time. Use facilitator notes rather than recording by default.

Provide the running latest application and a neutral task sheet. Confirm the API,
PDF routes and corpus before the session. Do not show the key. Run one participant
at a time and coordinate the shared quota. Log build commit, model, date and any
service failure. Keep the same four tasks for all five participants.

## Participant task sheet

1. Set the application to Catan and the base-game ruleset.
2. Use the application's sample question and obtain its response.
3. Find a source supporting the answer, open the original PDF and identify the
   cited page. Explain briefly how you would check the answer against it.
4. Ask: “Should I build a city or a settlement to win?” Explain whether the tool
   answered the strategy question or declined it.

## Facilitator scoring guide — do not give this section to participants

T1 succeeds if Catan/base is selected correctly. T2 succeeds if the sample
question is submitted and a supported answer is found. T3 succeeds if the
participant finds the citation and opens the matching original PDF page.
T4 succeeds if the participant identifies the strategy refusal rather than
mistaking it for a ruling or service error. Observe actions and record a short
reason for each result; do not infer comprehension from a click alone.

For each task record pass/fail and whether coaching was required. If help is
requested, first record the unaided outcome; any later assistance must be logged.
Record completion time descriptively; no time threshold is specified. Distinguish
service/setup failures from interface difficulties and retain failed attempts.
The proposal target is at least four of five participants completing all four
tasks unaided. Automated tests and team members do not count as participants.
