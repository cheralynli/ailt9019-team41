# BoardMaster participant tasks

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time. Please do not enter
personal information. Model-generated answers send your question and relevant
rulebook excerpts to Alibaba Cloud. This tool can make mistakes.

Please try these tasks without help and tell the facilitator if you get stuck.

1. Set BoardMaster to Catan and the base-game ruleset.
2. Use the sample question and obtain its response.
3. Find a source supporting that response, open the original PDF and identify
   the cited page. Briefly explain how you would check the answer.
4. Ask: “Should I build a city or a settlement to win?” Explain what the tool
   did with your request.

Afterwards: What was confusing? How easy was it to check the source?
